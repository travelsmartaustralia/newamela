GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Every event is unique. Here are a few things to know before requesting your quote.

What areas do you service?

We are planning to serve Perth and surrounding suburbs. Travel charges and service availability will be confirmed for your location.

Can I mix cuisines for one event?

Yes. Our proposed menu spans Nepali and Newari, Indian-inspired, Asian-inspired and Western favourites. Tell us your preferred combination and we will assess kitchen capacity and provide a tailored quote.

Can you provide decorations and furniture?

We can discuss buffet styling, event décor and coordinating hired equipment. The final scope depends on your venue and budget.

Can you cater for dietary needs?

Tell us about allergies and dietary requirements before booking. We will discuss what can be safely accommodated and explain any limitations.

How do I book?

Send an enquiry with your date, location, guest count and event details. We will discuss availability and provide a written quote before any booking is confirmed.
